# Arrabiatain

## Ingredients
- 1 cup of Pasta
- 1/2 tsp of Olive oil
- Salt
- Water
- 2 cloves of Garlic
- 1 tsp of Butter
- 1/2 cup of Tomato Puree
- 1 tsp Oregano
- 1/3 cup of Mozzarella

## Instructions
- Boil pasta in water with salt and 1/2 tsp of olive oil.
- Strain the pasta when it's almost cooked.
- Add garlic and butter in a pan.
- Later add tomato puree with salt.
- Then add Pasta.
- Garnish it with Oregano and add grated Mozzarella.
